The Problem

The internet is full of answers. Trust is the missing layer.

Social media rewards the loudest voice. AI gives confident answers without lived experience. Experts are buried under noise. Opinion is built for a different kind of conversation.

Loudest voice wins

Social media rewards volume and virality, not accuracy or lived experience.

Confident, but hollow

AI gives fast, confident answers — without the lived experience behind them.

Experts, buried

Real expertise gets lost under noisy comment sections and shallow surveys.

The Solution

Turn scattered opinions into structured insight.

Ask an important question. Invite people to share what they think. Bring in verified experts to add context. Let AI organise the discussion into themes, agreement, disagreement and evidence-backed insight.

  • 2 Context and credibility from verified experts
  • 3 Clarity from AI-organised themes and consensus

Contribution Quality

Designed to reward better thinking.

Not every opinion carries the same value in every discussion. Opinion helps identify contributions that are clear, relevant, reasoned, evidence-backed and useful to the question being asked.

Instead of rewarding noise, virality or confidence, Opinion highlights the ideas that help people understand the issue better.

  • Clarity & relevance
  • Reasoning & evidence
  • Lived experience & expert input
  • Community feedback
We score contribution quality — never people. Opinion never ranks or scores individuals.
